Task category patterns

Findings below are computed directly from the CSAT-completed data (Hyphen + mCaffeine, all resolvers unless noted) — not from the filters above.

  • undelivered order is the single weakest category by volume — avg 2.99 across 1,418 completed CSATs, well below the 3.98 overall average. coupon_code_error (3.12, n=481), delay_delivery (3.27, n=1,944) round out the lowest-rated categories with meaningful sample size.
  • AI trails Human by roughly a point or more on categories that need judgment or account correction — damaged_product_order (AI 2.32/n=69 vs Human 4.51/n=2,085), wrong_product_dispatched (AI 2.39/n=44 vs Human 4.35/n=787), refund not recieved (AI 2.65/n=624 vs Human 4.14/n=1,775) and wrongly_marked_delivered (AI 2.85/n=75 vs Human 4.30/n=1,174) all show a wide gap with enough volume on both sides that this isn't noise. These are exactly the categories where a wrong automated answer — a refund status, a dispatch claim, a delivery mark — is verifiably wrong to the customer.
  • On the single largest category, Estimated_delivery_date (n=15,358, 24.2% of all responses), AI (3.86) is essentially level with Human (3.84) — a pure status lookup is where AI performs closest to parity. AI also leads Human on 'rating customer service' (4.40 vs 4.12, n=3,288 AI). The AI shortfall looks concentrated in categories needing account changes or judgment calls, not simple lookups.
  • out_for_delivery for AI-resolved tickets fell from 3.65 (n=258) in Mar 2026 to 2.69 (n=294) in Jul 2026 — a 0.95-point drop on comparable volume. change in order detail shows a similar slide (4.42 → 3.50, drop 0.92), as does unable_see_1 (4.04 → 3.18). Worth investigating what changed in these AI flows over the period.

All AI-vs-Human comparisons use a minimum sample size of n≥30 per side to avoid drawing conclusions from a handful of responses.
