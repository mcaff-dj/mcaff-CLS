Reference guide

Onboarding Test

Customer support scenarios and the expected course of action for each — use this as a reference while handling calls/tickets, or to test your understanding of process during onboarding.

1. A customer added only one product during a "Buy 1 Get 1" offer. It is her birthday and her first order. What should be done?

Expected answerWe should clear how B1G1 works, and since this is a birthday gift, we should give them one product.

2. A customer claims a delivery boy abused and misbehaved with her. What should be done?

Expected answerCall needed, then mail the delivery team about this, and we can provide a 50% refund for such cases as an exception.

3. A customer is unable to apply the coupon "B2799" on Power Peptide. What should be done?

Expected answerThis coupon code is not valid on this product.

4. A customer mentioned that the Google coupon code for "Buy 2 Get 7" is not working, even though the "Buy 3 at 999" offer is already live on the site. (a) What should be done if the coupon code provided was wrong? (b) What should be done if the customer is applying it incorrectly?

Expected answerNeed to check the coupon first — if the coupon was wrong, escalate it; if they are applying it incorrectly, we should assist them.

5. A customer mentioned the product is not effective after using it for two months and has reached out on different platforms. What should be done?

Expected answerCall and check what happened, then help with an alternate product.

6. A customer mentioned they added two products to their cart and wants to know why they didn’t receive three free products. What should be done?

Expected answerWe should explain how B2G3 works, and as an exception, we can help with one freebie.

7. A customer ordered from Amazon and the product caused a skin reaction (acne/pimples). What should be done?

Expected answerWe should call and try to pitch another product, and if the customer is adamant, we should refund.

8. A customer ordered from Blinkit and the products arrived totally broken. What should be done?

Expected answerWe should ask them to reach Blinkit support, and if Blinkit denies helping, we will ask for a screenshot of that and then resolve it ourselves.

9. A customer purchased body lotion but cannot use it because the pump is missing or not working. What should be done?

Expected answerWe should first ask for an image/video, then check whether the customer is using it correctly or not — if it's a genuine issue, we resolve it.

10. A customer reached out regarding a refund not reflecting in their bank account. We shared screenshots from all three platforms, but the customer has now shared their UPI statement with us. What should be done?

Expected answerSince the customer is sharing UPI history, we will need the bank statement instead — sometimes a refund doesn't reflect in UPI right away. We'll clarify this to the customer.

11. A customer reached out with a screenshot of a payment issue, mentioning they haven’t received an order ID or a refund yet. What should be done?

Expected answerFirst check the screenshot to confirm payment was made to us, then check with POC and verify whether the order was dropped, auto-refunded, or actually placed.

12. A customer shared an unboxing video showing she received Mamaearth products in her box instead of ours. What should be done?

Expected answerIf it's the customer's first time raising this, we should provide a complimentary replacement; if it's a repeat occurrence, we ask them to wait while we review the footage.

13. A customer who has spoken to 4+ agents is now assigned to you; they are highly frustrated and asking for a freebie (Stanley). What should be done?

Expected answerWe should clarify to them that we're not able to share that, and then offer a freebie as a very special exception.

14. An order has been delayed for over 8 days and the customer has reached out to our founder to raise a formal complaint. What should be done?

Expected answerWe should prioritize the shipment, and can also offer a freebie and coupon; as a very special exception, a half refund can be given after checking with POC.

15. An order was placed on 1 June and the status is still reflecting as "Pickup Generated." What should be done?

Expected answerRaise a PE (pickup exception) case, and we should reorder.

16. An order was placed on 14 April (RTO) and the customer is now asking for a refund. What should be done?

Expected answerSince it was COD and the order is RTO (delivery returned to origin), a refund is needed.

17. If a customer mentions they are trying to apply a Google Form coupon code but are unable to do so, what should be done?

Expected answerWe should ask them to share a screenshot, then clarify that the coupon code is applicable only on the products listed below the coupon code — if the same issue occurs even after this, escalate it.

18. If an order was split (two products), one was delivered and the second was partially dispatched. The order was COD and the customer reached out after 25 days. What should be done?

Expected answerA partial NDR/NSZ case isn't possible in this scenario — this question is flagged as not applicable / incorrectly framed.

19. We mistakenly promised a customer they would receive a Stanley, and now they have reached out to us over Twitter. What should be done?

Expected answerWe should clarify to them that we're not able to share that, and offer something as a very special exception.

20. What is the difference between "Buy 2 Get 3" and "Buy 2 Get 5"?

Expected answerBoth are the same offer, just marketed under different names.
